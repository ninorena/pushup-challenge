# Release notes

What changed on the site, newest first. The newest entry also shows in the "What's new" box on the page (`whats-new.js`).

## 2026-11-21: Retest any day

You can now do a max-reps test on any day, and your target changes that same day.

- The days before your test keep your old target. The days from your test on use the new one.
- Example: your target is 130 a week. On Thursday you test 30, which is 195 a week. This week's target is 167. Next week it is 195.
- If your first test is mid-week, you get a target for the rest of that week, and you count in the group goal at once.
- A test in week 4, the light week, still starts the next week.

## 2026-11-16: Season 1 starts today

Season 1 starts today, Monday, Nov 16.

- If you logged a max-reps test before today, your target starts this week.
- If you have not done a test yet, test this week. Your target starts next Monday.
- Week 4 of season 1 is Dec 7 to 13. It is a light week. Retest in that week.

## 2026-11-11: Max-reps test and seasons

Your target now comes from your own max-reps test, not from your first week.

Before Monday, Nov 23, do one set of as many good pushups as you can. Log it, and tick the max-reps test box. Do the test in the kind of pushup that you will train in: wall, box, knee or full.

- Your weekly target is your test times 6.5. A test of 20 gives 130 a week.
- Most sets are not max sets. Do about two-thirds of your max, and stop before it gets hard.
- A season is 4 weeks. Week 4 is a light week at half your target. Retest in that week.
- The group goal is the average percent of target. Go over 100% to cover a friend.
- Coming back is a win. When you log after 3 or more days off, you get a ↩ next to your name for that day.
- Your old reps stay in Past weeks.
